# Demo Script — 3 minutes / 演示逐字稿（3 分钟）

Time budget: intro 25s · demo 1m50s · close 35s. Speak, don't read.
时间分配：开场 25 秒 · 演示 1 分 50 秒 · 收尾 35 秒。照意思讲，不要念稿。

---

## 0:00 — Open / 开场 (25s)

**EN** — Planning an event means calling five different vendors and getting five all-day quotes. Most people don't need a photographer for eight hours. They need three, around the ceremony. This app lets you book every part of an event by the hour, and watch the total against your budget while you do it.

**中** — 办一场活动，通常要联系五家供应商，拿回五份整天报价。可是大多数人并不需要摄影师待八小时，只需要仪式前后的三小时。这个产品让你按小时预订活动的每一部分，并在过程中实时看到总价与预算的对比。

---

## 0:25 — Set the frame / 设定框架 (20s)

*(Plan screen)*

**EN** — I start with the frame: a wedding, September 12th, Lisbon, 60 guests, three thousand dollars. Below that are the five things an event needs — venue, photographer, DJ, makeup, chef — each showing its lowest hourly rate.

**中** — 先设定框架：婚礼、9 月 12 日、里斯本、60 人、预算 3000 美元。下面是活动需要的五个部分——场地、摄影、DJ、化妆、主厨——每项显示最低小时价。

---

## 0:45 — Compare and book / 比价与预订 (45s)

*(Browse → Vendor detail)*

**EN** — Photographers. I compare hourly rates side by side, with a three-hour reference price so the numbers are comparable. I'll open Amara — bio, reviews, and the part that matters: hours. Three hours, three hundred and sixty dollars. Push it to four, and the estimate moves with it. I pick a four o'clock start and add her to the event. Notice the bar at the bottom always tells me what's left of the budget.

**中** — 进入摄影师列表。小时费率并排比较，并给出 3 小时参考价，让数字可比。打开 Amara——介绍、评价，以及最关键的：小时数。3 小时是 360 美元；加到 4 小时，估价随之变化。我选 4 点开始，加入活动。注意底部一直显示预算剩余多少。

---

## 1:30 — Fill the plan / 补齐清单 (35s)

*(Add venue + chef quickly)*

**EN** — Same pattern for everything else. A venue for five hours. A chef for three. Each category keeps one vendor, so choosing again simply replaces the last one — there's nothing to undo.

**中** — 其他品类同理。场地 5 小时，主厨 3 小时。每个品类只保留一位供应商，重新选择直接替换，无需撤销操作。

---

## 2:05 — The budget view / 预算视图 (35s)

*(My event)*

**EN** — This is the whole event on one screen: what I booked, for how many hours, what each part costs. The bar shows how much of the budget is used, and it tells me the two categories I still haven't filled. If I go over, the number turns red — it warns me, it doesn't stop me. One tap sends enquiries to every vendor at once.

**中** — 一屏看完整场活动：预订了什么、多少小时、各自多少钱。进度条显示预算使用比例，并提示还有两个品类没有填。如果超支，数字变红——只是提示，不会阻止。一键向所有供应商同时发送询价。

---

## 2:40 — Close / 收尾 (20s)

**EN** — Five vendors, one budget, one screen. It's deployed and live at the link I've shared. Next steps would be a vendor side for real availability, and payment — but the customer flow works end to end today.

**中** — 五类供应商、一个预算、一个界面。已经部署上线，链接已分享。下一步是做供应商端来接入真实排期，以及支付；但客户端流程今天已经完整跑通。

---

## If asked / 常见追问

- **Where's the data from?** Sample data in this build; the structure is ready for a real vendor source.
  **数据来源？** 当前为示例数据，结构已可对接真实供应商数据源。
- **Real bookings?** It sends enquiries. Payment is deliberately out of scope for v1.
  **是否真实下单？** 目前只发询价，支付在 v1 中有意不做。
- **Why hourly?** It's the whole point — it's what makes the budget controllable.
  **为什么按小时？** 这正是核心，让预算变得可控。
